## Methods of Communication

The primary method of communication with Mac Center is **9.032 (VHF alias 128.700 MHz)** .

Aircraft approaching Antarctica will normally transfer to **118.200 (VHF)** whilst operating within the McMurdo Sector.

!!! warning "Use of 118.200 (VHF)"
    Controllers must cross-couple (`XC`) **NZCM_CTR** to transmit on **118.200 (VHF)**.

    **NZCM_CTR** is a utility position used only for cross-coupling and must **never** be staffed.

## Airspace Services

### Class A

Class A airspace extends from `FL245` to `FL600` throughout the McMurdo Sector.

Mac Center provides standard enroute and oceanic ATC services within this airspace.

### Class D

When **NZFX_TWR** is online, the airspace within **4.3 NM** of Phoenix Field from the surface to `A025` is Class D.

Aircraft shall be transferred to **NZFX_TWR** prior to entering Class D airspace.

### Class E

Class E airspace extends from the surface to `FL245` within **100 NM** of Pegasus Field and Williams Field, excluding Class D airspace.

Within Class E airspace:

- IFR aircraft shall be separated from other IFR aircraft.
- IFR aircraft shall receive traffic information on VFR aircraft where practicable.
- VFR aircraft may request Flight Following.
- Traffic and hazard information should be provided whenever practicable.

!!! note
    VFR aircraft do **not** require an ATC clearance to enter Class E airspace.

## Transfer of Control

### ARO

Aircraft leaving the McMurdo Sector for **ARO** shall be instructed to contact Auckland Radio at the designated transfer point.

!!! example "Transfer to ARO"

    **Mac Center**: *"ANZ123, at `WAYPOINT`, contact Auckland Radio on 129.000."*

    **ANZ123**: *"At `WAYPOINT`, Auckland Radio 128.700, ANZ123."*

### BNE

Aircraft leaving the McMurdo Sector for **IND** shall be instructed to contact Melbourne Radio at the designated transfer point.

!!! example "Transfer to BNE"

    **Mac Center**: *"QFA27, at `WAYPOINT`, contact Melbourne Radio on 124.650."*

    **QFA27**: *"At `WAYPOINT`, Melbourne Radio on 124.650, QFA27."*

## Coordination

### Aerodrome Operations

When **NZWD_APP** is online, aircraft shall be transferred prior to entering Class D airspace.

When **NZFX_TWR** is online, arrivals and departures should be coordinated as required.

!!! example "Coordination"

    <span class="coldline">**NZCM** -> **NZFX**</span>: "ANZ123, inbound Phoenix Field, FL250, estimating `PEGAS` at 0215"

    <span class="coldline">**NZFX** -> **NZCM**</span>: "ANZ123, `PEGAS` 0215 FL250"

---

## Non-Radar Approach Service

!!! warning
    When no aerodrome controller is online, Mac Center may provide a non-radar approach service within Class E airspace.

    Mac Center shall **not** provide a control service within Class D airspace.

---

## Search and Rescue

Mac Center provides Search and Rescue coordination for aircraft operating within the McMurdo Sector.

Controllers should maintain situational awareness of overdue or missing aircraft and coordinate with neighbouring ATS units where required.
