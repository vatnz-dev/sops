### Event Only Positions

!!! Danger "Important"
    The following are designated as Event Only positions, and may only be staffed during a VATNZ event where approved, or if explicitly authorised by the Operations Director.

| Position Name  | Shortcode | Callsign         | Frequency | Login ID | Usage      |
| -------------- | --------- | ---------------- | --------- | -------- | ---------- |
| Ice Runway ADC | TIR       | Ice Runway Tower | 126.200   | NZIR_TWR | Event Only |

!!! note "NZWD_APP coverage"
    `NZIR_TWR` is an event only aerodrome position.

    `NZWD_APP` is not event only and provides the overlying approach service for the Williams Field terminal area, including traffic operating to and from Ice Runway.

## Responsibilities

Ice Runway Tower is responsible for local aerodrome operations at NZIR when the position is staffed for an event. This includes runway operations, local traffic sequencing, and issuing takeoff and landing clearances.

`NZWD_APP` remains responsible for the surrounding terminal area, IFR sequencing, approach clearances, departure releases, and onward coordination with `NZCM_FSS`.

When `NZIR_TWR` is not online, `NZWD_APP` provides top-down service for NZIR.

## Operations

### Departures

Departing IFR aircraft require a release from `NZWD_APP` before takeoff unless otherwise coordinated. Once airborne and clear of immediate aerodrome traffic, transfer aircraft to `NZWD_APP`.

### Arrivals

Arriving aircraft should be sequenced by `NZWD_APP` and transferred to Ice Runway Tower for landing clearance once established or when otherwise coordinated.

Visual approaches may be accepted when traffic and conditions permit.

## Coordination

### NZIR_TWR to NZWD_APP

Coordinate IFR departure releases, arrival sequencing, runway changes, missed approaches, and any non-standard operation with `NZWD_APP`.

### No Overlying Controller

When `NZWD_APP` is not online, coordinate with `NZCM_FSS` if available. If no overlying controller is online, transfer aircraft to UNICOM on `122.800` once no further tower service is required.
